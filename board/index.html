Status bar

Your 5-hour and weekly limits, what you spent today and the agents at work, in the look of the theme you picked.

Side panel

The radar, the tool mix and your notes. Turned off, its room goes to the Claude windows and terminals. Your notes are kept.

Chat

Off, nothing is lost: the notes stay in the conversation and show again when you turn this on.

Caveman mode

Claude answers tersely to save tokens, with the caveman skill's rules. Switch it for one session with the rock button in its title bar. It starts with your next message to that session. Code, commits and warnings stay written normally.

Layout

Drag a card by its title bar to move it: the cards in its way make room, and every card stays where you leave it. Drop a card into an empty space and it fills it. Hover an empty space for a + that starts something right there. Drag a card's side, bottom or corner to resize it.

Close the gaps packs the cards together again, top first, keeping their sizes. Reset layout puts every card back, two to a row.

Scrolling

Off, scrolling works as on any web page: a chat scrolls until its end, and then the board does.

Scroll buttons

Shift is there to start with. Click Add button, then press the mouse button or key to add, like an extra button on the side of your mouse. Remove any with its ×, as long as one is left.

Editor

On, the file name in an opened step (an edit, a read, a new file) opens that file in your editor at the line Claude changed or read.
